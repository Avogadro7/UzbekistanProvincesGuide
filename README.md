# 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan Provinces Guide

An educational mobile app that provides information about all provinces (viloyatlar) of Uzbekistan. Learn about the history, culture, and beauty of Uzbek regions in three languages: Uzbek, Russian, and English.

## Features

### 📚 Comprehensive Information
- **All 14 regions** - Complete coverage of Uzbekistan provinces
- **Major cities** - Information about key cities in each region
- **Beautiful images** - High-quality photos of landmarks and attractions
- **Historical facts** - Learn about the rich history of each province

### 🌍 Multi-Language Support
- 🇺🇿 **O'zbek tili** - Uzbek language
- 🇷🇺 **Русский язык** - Russian language
- 🇬🇧 **English** - English language

### 🏛️ Covered Regions
- **Toshkent** - Capital and largest city
- **Samarqand** - Ancient city on the Silk Road
- **Andijon** - Major city in Fergana Valley
- **Buxoro** - Historic city with UNESCO sites
- **Farg'ona** - Fergana Valley region
- **Namangan** - Industrial and agricultural center
- **Qashqadaryo** - Southern province
- **Surxondaryo** - Southernmost region
- **Sirdaryo** - Northern province
- **Jizzax** - Central region
- **Xorazm** - Ancient Khorezm region
- **Navoiy** - Desert province
- **Qoraqalpog'iston** - Autonomous republic

### 🎨 Beautiful Design
- Traditional Uzbek patterns and ornaments
- Golden color scheme representing Uzbek heritage
- Clean and intuitive interface
- Easy navigation between provinces

## Why This App?

I created this app because I wanted to share the beauty and history of my country with others. Many people don't know much about Uzbekistan's diverse regions, and this app helps discover the unique character of each province.
